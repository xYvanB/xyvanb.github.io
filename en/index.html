I’m a Full Stack Developer with 5+ years of experience, building B2B web applications for logistics and transport at The Bubble Company S.r.l. My day-to-day revolves around React, TypeScript, and Laravel — tools I’ve been growing with since my first React Native work back in 2020. Outside of client work, I tend to build things just because the problem interests me — a cross-platform Whisper transcription tool for non-technical users, WordPress automation pipelines with MCP and Figma, AI-powered workflows with Claude Code. If there’s a smarter way to do something, I’ll usually end up experimenting with it on a weekend. I’m based in Italy, work comfortably in both Italian and English professional contexts, and prefer environments where curiosity is treated as a feature, not a distraction.
